🪞 Golden-hour Lake Matheson — cost-effective & iconic

Swapped the paid kayak (~NZ$900+ group total) for a golden-hour walk at Lake Matheson. You get the iconic Aoraki/Mt Tasman mirror reflection at the best light of the day, plus Te Kopikopiko o te Waka's Ngāi Tahu glacier lookout on the same detour.

  • Free — save ~NZ$900+ for other experiences.
  • Golden hour timing — arrive Reflection Island ~18:00–19:00 for warm reflection light.
  • Proper dinner at SnakeBite by 19:30 (comfortably before 20:30 close).
  • Lower energy demand than 3 hrs of paddling after a 5-hr drive.

Pre-pack last night. Empty grey/black water, top up fresh. Quick breakfast in the van.

🍴 Midday · Haast & West Coast Drive

13:30 🍔 The Hard Antler, Haast — LUNCH Must60 min

Best meal between Wānaka and Franz Josef. Try the whitebait sandwich (West Coast specialty) or venison burger. Open 11am–10pm. Toilets across road. Big garlic butter on every steak.

📍 5 Marks Rd, Haast
14:30 🏛️ Awarua/Haast Visitor Centre Optional20 min

Small but well-curated museum on West Coast history & ecology. DOC staff can confirm SH6 road conditions ahead. Clean toilets.

📍 Open in Maps
15:00 🌳 Ship Creek Walk Optional25 min

Choose one loop: the sand dune walk along the Tasman, or the kahikatea swamp boardwalk through ancient forest. Both short, both stunning. Skip if running behind.

📍 Open in Maps
15:45 🌊 Knights Point Lookout Must15 min

First sweeping Tasman Sea view — dramatic rocky coastline and rolling forested headlands. Public toilets, large carpark. Quick leg-stretch before the ~90 min drive to Fox Glacier.

🏔️ Late Afternoon · Fox Glacier Detour

16:45 🛶 Te Kopikopiko o te Waka Main objective30 min

"The capsized canoe" — Ngāi Tahu cultural lookout developed in 2022. Turn onto Cook Flat Road just past Fox Glacier village. Panoramic Fox Glacier and Southern Alps view; carved waka tells the creation story of the South Island. Free. Late-afternoon sun still lights the glacier face.

📍 Cook Flat Rd, Fox Glacier
17:20 🪞 Lake Matheson — Full Loop Walk Main objective90 min

5 min drive from Te Kopikopiko. Cross the Clearwater suspension bridge, walk through ancient kahikatea and rimu forest. Full loop is 4.4 km, ~1.5 hrs. Key stops:
• Jetty Lookout (~20 min in) — first mirror view of Aoraki + Mt Tasman
• Reflection Island (~35 min in) — the iconic postcard shot
• View of Views (~45 min in) — elevated panorama at the top of the lake
Golden hour peaks ~18:30–19:00. Sunset at 20:30 — you'll be back at car park by 18:50 with warm light still on the peaks. Wind usually drops in the evening for better reflections.

📍 Lake Matheson car park
18:50 🚐 Depart Lake Matheson → Franz Josef 30 min drive

25 km north on SH6. Golden light on the drive if lucky. Total daylight remaining ~1.5 hrs.

🍽️ Evening · Franz Josef Village

19:20 🏕️ Check in: Franz Josef Top 10 Holiday Park Call ahead today20 min

Reception closes 17:00 — you arrive ~19:20. Must call +64 3 752 0735 today to arrange after-hours check-in and site number. Mountain-and-rainforest setting, immaculate facilities. Plug in, quick freshen up before dinner.

📍 2902 Franz Josef Highway
19:45 🍜 SnakeBite Brewery — DINNER Must75 min

Proper sit-down dinner. Well within 20:30 close — no more rushing. Asian fusion menu: panang curry, nasi goreng, calamari salad, local brews. Walk from Top 10 (~12 min) or short drive with parking on Main Rd. Book by phone if arriving after 19:30 on a busy Monday (+64 3 752 0234).

📍 28 Main Rd
21:15 😴 Return to Top 10 · Rest —

Early night. Day 11 is Franz Josef → Castle Hill via Hokitika (long day). Aim for lights out by 22:30.
